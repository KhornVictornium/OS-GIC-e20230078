--- Zombie and Orphan Cleanup Notes ---
Q: How are orphans cleaned up? A: When a parent exits early, init/systemd (PID 1) or a subreaper immediately adopts the orphan and calls wait() to reap its exit status once the orphan finishes.
Q: How are zombies cleaned up? A: A zombie is reaped when its parent calls wait() or waitpid() to read its exit status—or if the parent terminates without waiting, PID 1 adopts the zombie and reaps it.
Q: Can you kill a zombie with 'kill -9'? Why or why not? A: No. A zombie is already dead (terminated) and has no running code or memory left to receive signals; only its parent (or PID 1) can remove its process table entry via wait(), or you must kill the parent.
